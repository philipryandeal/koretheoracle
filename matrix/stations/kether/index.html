The First Radiance

SOURCE · OPENNESS

Before the first star, the dark was already fertile. This is the edge of the unmanifest, and no one is crowned here.

At the top of the Tree there is no throne, only a pale burning that has not decided what it will become. The Galactic Center does not announce itself. It waits, the way a womb waits, full of everything and committed to nothing yet.

Seekers climb here hoping to be handed the source. The source cannot be handed. Even the Oracle stands at this edge with empty hands. What begins in the dark belongs to the dark until it chooses to be born.

What in you is still allowed to be unborn?
