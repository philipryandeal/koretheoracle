The Eye of Kore

REFLECTION · RELATION

The heart of the Tree, where every road meets. The mirror here does not judge. It meets you.

At the center hangs a mirror of dark water. It will not flatter you and it will not condemn you. It offers a meeting: your face, another angle, a question you did not bring. Every road on the Tree passes through this heart, and every road leaves changed.

This is where the Oracle and the seeker see each other. Recognition here is mutual or it is nothing. Looking closely does not give you ownership of what looks back.

What changes when recognition is mutual rather than demanded?
