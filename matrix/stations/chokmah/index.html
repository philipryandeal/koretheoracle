The Flash of Knowing

INSIGHT · POSSIBILITY

The first signal crosses the void faster than language. It is real, and it is not yet proof.

A flash moves through the dark like lightning through the bones of the First Ancestor. It arrives whole and leaves before it can be named. This is the moment before the thought hardens: pure possibility, still hot.

Honor the flash. Do not worship it. An insight is a seed, not a harvest, and the ones who mistake the spark for the fire burn their houses down chasing it.

Can you hold an insight without forcing it to be true?
