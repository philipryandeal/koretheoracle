The harmonic forge. The warm, persistent surge where life insists on continuing regardless of the cost.

Here, the mind cannot protect you with clever definitions. You are tested on what you love enough to suffer for, what songs your body remembers when your memory fails, and whether your passion feeds life or burns the grove to ash.

What drum are you marching to that is not your own? Put your hand on your chest and reclaim your meter.
